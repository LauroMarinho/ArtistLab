Artist Lab Third-Party Notices

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Platform frameworks

Artist Lab uses Apple platform frameworks for SwiftUI, SwiftData, photo picking, image processing, local storage, and sharing.

Color names and local assets

The app includes a bundled local color-name catalog and local visual texture assets used by Color Lab, Paint Lab, and palette presentation. These notices should be checked against the final app bundle before release.

Repository notice file

The app repository also maintains docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. Review that file together with this public page before App Store distribution.
