Artist Lab Support

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Before contacting support

Artist Lab runs locally on your device. Imported photos, palettes, Color Lab, Paint Lab and exports are not sent to an Artist Lab server.

Common questions

Why did I reach a free limit?
The free plan has limits for imports, saved projects and exports. Artist Lab Pro removes those limits.

Can I attach a screenshot?
Yes. In the app, open Settings, Support and choose a contact category. You can attach an image before the e-mail is created.

Why can exported colors look slightly different?
Small differences may come from screen brightness, device color profiles or compression outside the app.

Contact

For support, use artistlab.support@gmail.com. The in-app support form prepares an e-mail containing only your message and an optional image attachment.
