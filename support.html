ArtistLab Support

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Before contacting support

ArtistLab runs locally on your device. Imported photos, palettes, Color Lab, Paint Lab and exports are not sent to an ArtistLab server.

Common questions

Why did I reach a free limit?
The free plan has limits for imports, saved projects and exports. ArtistLab Pro removes those limits.

Can I attach a screenshot?
Yes. In the app, open Settings, Support and choose a contact category. You can attach an image before the e-mail is created.

Why can exported colors look slightly different?
Small differences may come from screen brightness, device color profiles or compression outside the app.

Contact

For support, use artistlab.support@gmail.com. The in-app support form can prepare an e-mail with app version, system information and an optional image attachment.
